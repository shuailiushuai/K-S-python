================================================================================
ROOT CAUSE: Labor Matching Unit Mismatch
================================================================================

The critical bug was in the labor market matching algorithm:

BEFORE (WRONG):
    JO2 = max(0, L2d - employed_sector2)
         = max(0, 400 - 100)  # Comparing notional vs actual units!
         = 300                 # WRONG - treated as 300 actual workers

AFTER (CORRECT):
    employed_notional = employed_sector2 * Lscale
                      = 100 * 10 = 1000
    JO2 = max(0, L2d - employed_notional)
        = max(0, 400 - 1000)  # Now comparing same units
        = 0                    # CORRECT - no openings in period 1
        
    # Later periods:
    JO2 = max(0, 800 - 400) = 400  # Can hire more workers!
    actual_JO2 = 400 / 10 = 40     # Convert for matching

This fix enabled:
- Firms to expand employment as demand grows
- Production to scale with workforce
- GDP to grow dynamically
- Realistic unemployment dynamics
